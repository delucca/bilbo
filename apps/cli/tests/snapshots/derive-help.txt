bilbo derive: write notes about entries that stopped growing, with a model.

Usage:
  bilbo derive [--dry-run] [--redo] [--calls <n>] [<entry>...]

Options:
      --dry-run    Say what a run would send, and send nothing
      --redo       Redo what other settings wrote and what was passed over
      --calls <n>  Make at most this many calls

Kinds:
  session  bilbo ships an instruction for it
Providers:
  anthropic  the Anthropic API, with a key
  openai     the OpenAI API, with a key
  claude     the tool claude, with its own login and no key

Output:
  entries <n>, calls <n>, notes <n>, dropped <n>, refused <n>, failed <n>
  tokens in <n>, tokens out <n>, seconds <n>

Exit:
  0  done, also when derive is off, nothing is due or another derive runs
  1  a failed call, a refused entry, a busy or stopped provider, a store failure
  2  usage or config error

Examples:
  bilbo derive --dry-run

Docs: https://github.com/delucca/bilbo/wiki/Commands#derive
