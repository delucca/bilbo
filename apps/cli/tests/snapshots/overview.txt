bilbo keeps durable memory for coding agents: it remembers their sessions and
the pages they cite, and recalls them when a later session asks.

Usage: bilbo <verb> [<args>]

Verbs:
  remember  Keep files or a note as entries; a later run keeps what changed
  refresh   Keep what every follow gained or changed; a timer runs it
  recall    Search everything bilbo keeps and print the best chunks and notes
  digest    Answer a session's first prompt with what bilbo keeps on it
  read      Print an entry: its outline, or its records a page at a time
  tag       List the tags, or change the tags of entries
  forget    Take entries out of recall; what they held stays readable
  reindex   Rebuild the catalog and the search index from the store
  derive    Write notes about entries that stopped growing, with a model
  config    Show each setting, its value and where it came from

Run 'bilbo <verb> --help' for its options, output and exit codes.
Docs: https://github.com/delucca/bilbo/wiki
